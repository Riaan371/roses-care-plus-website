About Roses Care Plus

Based in Langebaan, Roses Care Plus is committed to delivering compassionate, person-centred care to individuals and families. From personal care and health monitoring to specialised packages for the elderly, palliative patients, and those living with dementia or disability — our experienced team is here to support you every step of the way, in the comfort of your own home.

Our Services

🛁

Personal Care

  • Bathing, showering, dressing & grooming assistance
  • Toileting and incontinence care support
  • Mobility support
  • Feeding assistance and meal preparation
  • Medication reminders and monitoring

Health & Wellness Support

  • Vital signs monitoring: Blood Pressure, Blood Glucose, Temperature
  • Post-hospital and post-surgery care

Home Support & Daily Living

Specialised Care Packages

  • Palliative Care / Hospice Support at Home
  • Dementia & Alzheimer's Care
  • Disability Care for both Adults and Minors
